# foxcade — Privacy Policy

foxcade generates and runs mini-games entirely on your device.

- **No data collection.** foxcade does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking.
- **On-device only.** Game generation runs locally in your browser — via the browser's built-in on-device AI where available, otherwise a local generator.
- **Only network request.** The optional "World Run" driving game fetches public vector map tiles from `openfreemap.org` to render a real map. This sends only map-tile coordinates — never any personal information. Every other game is fully offline.
- **Optional permissions** (on-device AI, notifications, tabs, sessions) are used solely to provide game features you activate, never to collect data.

Because foxcade collects no data, there is nothing to access, delete, or export.

Questions or concerns can be raised as an issue on the project's repository.
